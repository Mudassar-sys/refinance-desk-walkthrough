A short, friendly loan health check

Around three minutes. The caller gathers the facts your broker needs and books a time. You sign off the script before the first call.

  1. Open with who, where from and why"Hi [first name], it's [caller name] calling from [your brokerage] about your free loan health check. Just so you know, this call is recorded for your file. Is now an OK time?"
  2. Use the reason from the list"Our records show your fixed rate finishes in November, so we wanted to check in before it rolls over."
  3. Gather the facts"Roughly what's the balance now? What are your repayments, and how often? Is anything coming up, like renovating, buying again or wanting lower repayments?"
  4. Book the broker"Thanks, that's everything. One of our brokers will look at the options and talk you through it in plain English. Would Thursday at 10:30 or Friday at 2 suit?"
  5. If it's not a good time"No problem at all. When's better for you?" The callback goes straight into Mercury as a task.

What the caller never does

  • Name a lender or suggest a product
  • Quote a rate or promise a saving
  • Push a client who says no
  • Call anyone who has asked not to be called

Advice and recommendations stay with your licensed brokers. The caller's job is to find the clients who want to talk, and make the broker's first conversation an easy one.
